SECTION B - FIND THE ERROR
--------------------------
B1.  TypeError: can only concatenate str (not "int") to str
     Fix: print("I am " + str(age) + " years old")

B2.  ZeroDivisionError: division by zero
     You cannot divide by 0. Make sure the divisor is not 0.

B3.  NameError: name 'true' is not defined
     Fix: True with a capital T. Python is case-sensitive.

B4.  NameError: name 'Print' is not defined
     Fix: print in lowercase.

B5.  ValueError: invalid literal for int() with base 10: '3.5'
     int() cannot read text that has a decimal point.
     Fix: float("3.5"), or int(float("3.5")) to get 3.

B6.  SyntaxError. The ' inside It's ends the string early.
     Fix: print("It's sunny")  or  print('It\'s sunny')
