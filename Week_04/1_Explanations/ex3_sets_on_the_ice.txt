WEEK 4 - EXERCISE 3: WHO HAS BEEN OUT ON THE ICE?
=================================================
Solution code: 2_Solutions\ex3_sets_on_the_ice.py


WHAT THE QUESTION IS ASKING
---------------------------
Two groups of names:
    monday  = {"vera", "tom", "ines", "kofi"}
    tuesday = {"tom", "kofi", "lena"}

Answer 4 questions. Each answer must be ONE set expression:
  a) everyone who went out at least once
  b) who went out on both days
  c) who went out on Monday only
  d) who went out on exactly one of the two days


WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW
---------------------
A SET is a bag of things with:
  - no duplicates (each name appears once)
  - no order (so no index: monday[0] gives an error)
Curly brackets: {"vera", "tom"}

The 4 set operators. Draw two overlapping circles to picture them:

    monday | tuesday   UNION          everything in either circle
    monday & tuesday   INTERSECTION   only the overlap
    monday - tuesday   DIFFERENCE     left circle, minus the overlap
    monday ^ tuesday   SYMMETRIC DIFF both circles, minus the overlap

              MONDAY          TUESDAY
           ( vera  ( tom   )  lena )
           ( ines  ( kofi  )       )
                     ^overlap^


ANSWERS
-------
  a) monday | tuesday  -> {vera, tom, ines, kofi, lena}
  b) monday & tuesday  -> {tom, kofi}
  c) monday - tuesday  -> {vera, ines}
  d) monday ^ tuesday  -> {vera, ines, lena}


IMPORTANT
---------
Sets print in a RANDOM-LOOKING order. Your output can look like
{'kofi', 'tom'} or {'tom', 'kofi'}. Both are correct. Check the
contents, not the order.


COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
* Using + to join sets -> TypeError. Use | instead.
* Mixing up - and ^ :
    -  is ONE side only (Monday but not Tuesday)
    ^  is EITHER side, but not both
* Remember that tuesday - monday is different from monday - tuesday:
  it gives {lena}.
